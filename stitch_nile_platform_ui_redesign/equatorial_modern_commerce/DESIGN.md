## Brand & Style

This design system defines an elevated, trusted, and forward-looking e-commerce marketplace tailored for the dynamic Central African digital economy with a focus on Cameroon. Moving beyond generic retail tropes, the aesthetic synthesizes high-end editorial sophistication with localized operational credibility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Sovereign Trust & Stability:** Deep midnight navies establish unshakeable authority, security, and digital maturity, reassuring both informal merchants transitioning online and discerning urban consumers.
- **Vibrant Optimism & Warmth:** Glowing amber-gold highlights radiate African commercial energy, vibrancy, and prestige without slipping into chaotic discounting cues.
- **Effortless Clarity:** Layouts favor serene white space, crisp micro-borders, and impeccable typographic scale to instill confidence during checkout, local mobile payments, and delivery tracking.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Modern Commerce**:
- Clean geometric structure powered by balanced typographic hierarchies.
- Soft, atmospheric depth using midnight-tinted drop shadows rather than muddy greys.
- Hyper-tactile micro-interactions (magnetic action buttons, subtle pill active states, and crisp emerald verification badges) that make digital commerce feel reliable and frictionless across web and mobile viewports.

## Colors

The color palette builds visual hierarchy around security and high-intent actions:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** The structural bedrock. Used for top-level navigation headers, footer grounding, bold headlines, and primary high-priority states. Conveys enterprise reliability and institutional security.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`):** The catalytic conversion color. Applied intentionally to "Ajouter au panier", featured badge highlights, active promotional banners, and high-impact micro-triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** The verification signal. Reserved strictly for merchant trust mechanics ("Boutique validée"), safe escrow checkout indicators, positive inventory availability, and transaction confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` & `#FAFAF9`):** The luminous base canvas. Provides a soft, warm paper background that prevents eye fatigue in high-contrast tropical daylight and separates card surfaces naturally.
- **Named Local Payment Accents:** Dedicated hues (`#FFCC00` for MTN MoMo and `#FF6600` for Orange Money) are encapsulated inside neutral pill containers to maintain brand harmony while respecting recognized telecom payment identities.

## Typography

The design system unifies the interface with **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, bringing geometric clarity paired with humanist warmth. Its wide aperture, sharp terminal cuts, and distinct numeral forms prevent optical fatigue and ensure crisp rendering on varied DPI mobile displays.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Guidelines
- **Display & Headlines:** Headings use heavy weights (`700`, `800`) with tight optical tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor landing page storefronts and department headers with authoritative presence.
- **Commerce Numerals:** Currency labels (`FCFA`) use tabular number styling and bold weights to ensure price comparison is immediate and unambiguous across dense catalog rows.
- **Editorial Micro-copy:** Merchant verification, category chips, and process steps rely on uppercase micro-labels (`label-badge`) with positive letter-spacing (`+0.04em`) to establish crisp metadata separation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a standard 8pt grid with flexible responsive constraints designed for both mobile-first shoppers and multi-column desktop catalog browsing.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem` (`16px`), `gutter: 1rem` (`16px`). Product grids collapse into a tight 2-column browse card array or a horizontally swipeable carousel.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem` (`32px`), `gutter: 1.25rem` (`20px`). Adapts to 3-column product matrices and side-drawer facet filtering.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max container width `1360px`, `margin: 3.5rem` (`56px`), `gutter: 1.75rem` (`28px`). Accommodates fixed faceted sidebar navigation, 4-column product grids, and wide merchandising split banners.

### Spacing Density Principles
- **Internal Card Density:** Component padding remains disciplined (`space-md` on mobile, `space-lg` on desktop) to retain high information density while letting product photography breathe.
- **Section Breaks:** Major landing segments (Hero, "Coups de cœur", "Boutiques vérifiées", and Trust modules) utilize `space-2xl` (`64px`) to create clear thematic pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** combined with **Ambient Navy-Tinted Shadows** rather than flat black drop shadows. This gives surfaces a crisp, three-dimensional physical presence.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Surface neutral `#FAFAF9` or `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Flat Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Soft ambient glow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards on Hover & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward lift (`translateY(-3px)`), with enriched shadow: `0px 10px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Search Suggestion Drawers, Cart Previews):** High surface presence: `0px 20px 30px -10px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0px 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`.
- **Hero & Merchant Accent Glow:** Key conversion modules and promotional badges cast a directional ambient amber aura: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The system uses a balanced **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape language. This avoids harsh, dated corporate rectangles while preserving structural precision across compact data points.

### Shape Scale
- **Small Corners (`rounded-md`, 6px):** Checkboxes, form helper badges, quantity toggles, and compact dropdown menus.
- **Standard Radius (`rounded-lg`, 12px - 16px):** Primary interactive buttons, product image containers, input fields, and standard commerce cards.
- **Prominent Radius (`rounded-xl`, 20px - 24px):** Large promotional hero containers, merchant showcase banners, and modal sheets.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Category pill chips, "Boutique validée" status badges, floating WhatsApp support triggers, and search bar hulls.

## Components

### 1. Unified Search Architecture
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) dual-tone layout. Left icon search indicator, clean placeholder (`Que recherchez-vous aujourd'hui ?`), and an integrated amber action button (`#F59E0B`) with an interactive magnifying glass icon.
- **States:** Focus produces an outer focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`) and opens a floating Level 3 elevated predictive drawer showing recent terms, trending categories, and verified merchant shortcuts.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Ajouter", "Acheter"):** Solid amber (`#F59E0B`) with navy text (`#0B192C`) or high-contrast white, medium-bold (`600`), `rounded-lg` or `rounded-full` depending on placement. Hover transitions to `#D97706` with subtle scale lift.
- **Merchant / Secondary Action ("Explorer", "Fiche", "Vendre"):** Deep midnight navy (`#0B192C`) fill with white text, or pure white background with subtle border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and midnight text.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `1px solid transparent`, `#0B192C` text with inline chevron icon for directional browsing.

### 3. Commerce Product Cards
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-xl` shape, subtle ambient shadow.
- **Visual Section:** Centered product imagery encased in a soft neutral framed background (`#F8FAFC`), isolated from edge borders with `space-sm` padding.
- **Metadata Hierarchy:**
  1. Department/Category tag in muted uppercase (`11px`, `#64748B`).
  2. Product title (`15px`, bold, `#0B192C`, max 2-line clamp).
  3. Star rating or "Pas encore noté" indicator.
  4. Primary price in FCFA (`label-price-lg`, bold `#0B192C`).
  5. Merchant ownership row with trust indicator.
- **Card Actions:** Split-button row featuring rapid "Ajouter" CTA and secondary "Fiche" quick-view button.

### 4. Trust Badges & Verification Elements
- **"Boutique Validée":** Emerald soft container (`#ECFDF5`), emerald border (`#A7F3D0`), dark emerald typography (`#047857`), paired with an emerald checkmark icon. Displayed on cards and merchant profile headers.
- **Local Payment Strip:** Compact footer and checkout pill display featuring MTN MoMo and Orange Money icons encased in clean white rounded plates with crisp micro-borders.

### 5. Form Controls & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Height `46px`, `rounded-lg`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. On active focus: border transitions to `#0B192C` or `#F59E0B` with zero harsh outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` touch target, rounded corners (`4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios). Checked state fills with `#0B192C` accompanied by crisp white icon ticks.

### 6. Chips & Filtering Pills
- **Category Filter Chips:** Full-pill (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding. Inactive state: white background with `#E2E8F0` border. Active state: `#0B192C` fill with clean white text and clear removal trigger.
